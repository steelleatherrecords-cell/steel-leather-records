WALLOP – METALLIC ALPS

Germany, 1985 · The sound of an uncompromising era

There was a time when Heavy Metal was loud, hungry and unapologetic.

A time of leather jackets, denim, long hair, battered amplifiers and rehearsal rooms filled with the sound of guitars turned up to eleven. Weekends meant gigs, cold beer, new records and nights that seemed to have no end. Somewhere between the local metal club and the next rehearsal, young musicians were building their own world — one riff at a time.

This was the atmosphere of the mid-1980s. Across Germany and the rest of Europe, Heavy Metal was alive with ambition. Bands were pushing their sound, chasing bigger stages and dreaming of making their mark. There were no guarantees, no shortcuts and certainly no promises of success. Just music, determination and the desire to play as loud as humanly possible.

WALLOP were part of that world.

Released in 1985, Metallic Alps stands as a genuine piece of German Heavy Metal from that era. A record shaped by the energy and attitude of a generation that lived for its music. No elaborate concepts, no unnecessary decoration — just the unmistakable spirit of a band with its own sound and something to say.

Records like this remind us of what made the underground Heavy Metal scene so special. The excitement of discovering an obscure band, the thrill of hearing a killer riff for the first time, the friendships formed around records and concerts, and the feeling that somewhere out there, another great band was waiting to be discovered.

Metallic Alps belongs to that tradition.

It takes us back to a time when a band's reputation could travel from one fan to another, from one concert to the next, and when finding a forgotten Heavy Metal record felt like striking gold.

Four decades later, its spirit remains intact.

This is not about looking back and pretending that everything was perfect. It is about recognising the passion, the attitude and the music that made those years unforgettable.
